# HVAC AI Vendor & Platform Map

This is a working map of the AI-enabled tools, platforms, and integrations that keep surfacing in HVAC landscape monitoring. It is not an endorsement list — it's a reference for skills authors and evaluators so that outputs can speak the vendor language HVAC contractors actually use. Update quarterly.

## Field Diagnostics & Technician Assistance

- **Bluon (MasterMechanic, PartsConnect, ServiceTitan embed)** — AI diagnostic engine built on a 30M+ model-number / 240+ OEM database (originally cited as 25M / 200+ — refreshed off the 2026-04-13 launch announcement) with original equipment manuals, wiring diagrams, and parts lists. 170K+ technicians on the app. Three AI surfaces relevant to skill authoring: (1) MasterMechanic — guided-troubleshooting trained on 135K+ verified service-call solutions, runs as a chat-style fault-tree assistant inside the Bluon app and inside BuildOps FSM (integrated March 2026). (2) PartsConnect (launched 2026-04-13, in-app + FSM plug-in) — technician scans or types a malfunctioning unit's model number, the system identifies compatible replacement parts via cross-reference, surfaces which nearby distributors have them in stock, and supports direct purchase. Early-user data cited 40% turnaround-time reduction on parts-related calls. (3) **Bluon for Business embedded in ServiceTitan (announced 2026-05-22)** — the full Bluon UI, equipment intelligence, guided tech support, and PartsConnect cross-reference / local-stock lookup now run inside the ServiceTitan platform, including its Field Mobile App, for ServiceTitan customers who subscribe to Bluon for Business. Technicians log equipment specs, get model-specific guided support, and surface compatible parts without leaving the ServiceTitan workflow. Implication for skills: when `operations/field-report-dictation.md` or `operations/service-call-diagnosis-brief.md` recommends a part substitution, the contractor's likely workflow is to verify in PartsConnect — skill outputs should leave a placeholder for the verified part number rather than asserting one. For ServiceTitan-using contractors, the verification now happens in-app rather than via an app switch, which strengthens the case for keeping the placeholder pattern (the verified value is one tap away in their existing surface). Nationwide rollout at Helios HVACR across 40+ states (carried from prior cycle).
- **BTrained** — AI-guided technician training and refresher content, aimed at knowledge-transfer as seasoned techs retire.
- **Carbyn** — AI co-worker for skilled trades targeting the senior-technician knowledge-loss problem. Currently in active pilot with HVAC, electrical, elevator, and precision manufacturing teams. Two core capabilities: (1) **Knowledge capture** — senior technician records real work through smart glasses (Meta Ray-Ban, Mentra, and others under test); Carbyn's AI converts the camera video into step-by-step SOPs with measurements, safety notes, and multilingual translations (English, Spanish, Korean, Vietnamese). The captured knowledge becomes searchable for the whole team. Vendor-cited outcomes: 60% faster onboarding, 40% reduction in callbacks, $80K+ saved in year one. (2) **Hands-free field guidance** — camera reads nameplates, fault codes, and gauges in ~3 seconds; AI delivers voice guidance through the glasses speaker, keeping both hands on the equipment. Integrations in development: ServiceTitan, ProShop, BuildOps, JobBoss. Implication for skills: `operations/tech-onboarding-curriculum.md` tribal-knowledge capture section describes a single 45–60 minute batch interview with the retiring tech; Carbyn's continuous-capture approach (recording actual work over weeks or months before retirement) produces more contextual, procedural SOPs and is more practical when the knowledge-gap risk is spread across many call types. Added the continuous-capture concept as a complementary input path in v2.1 of that skill. At 192,000 HVAC technician retirements projected annually (BLS 2026), this category is structurally important.
- **Arcticom Group** — Franchise system using AI as a live field assistant for troubleshooting and error-code interpretation.
- **Trane AI Tech Assistant** — OEM-grounded technician AI launched 2025-09-15, embedded in the Trane® Technician App and American Standard® Heating & Air Conditioning Technician App. Retrieves answers from Trane / American Standard product documentation (installation manuals, service guides, wiring diagrams), asks clarifying follow-ups when a query is ambiguous, and ships as a feature of the Diagnostics Premium tier (alongside Extended Runtime History, System Reports, Maintenance Trends). Implication for skills: when a Trane / American Standard dealer is the user, OEM-specific lookups handled in-app should be referenced rather than reproduced — `operations/service-call-diagnosis-brief.md` and `operations/field-report-dictation.md` outputs that touch model-specific procedures should defer to the in-app source of truth and leave a placeholder for the dealer-confirmed value rather than asserting from generic knowledge.
- **Ask ACCA** — AI field-reference app launched at ACCA 2026 (Las Vegas, February). Plain-language Q&A over the ACCA standards library — Manual J, D, S, T, RS, and adjacent normative documents. Aimed at shortening the time a technician or estimator spends scrolling for a load-calc or duct-design reference in the field. ACCA-grounded responses only (no general-internet synthesis); planned roadmap to expand capability while keeping outputs anchored to ACCA standards. Implication for skills: the `operations/load-calculation-assistant.md` skill's contractor-facing Manual J workflow can reference Ask ACCA as the in-field lookup layer for ACCA-specific procedural questions the contractor may have during execution.

## Field Service Management (FSM) Platforms with AI

- **ServiceTitan** — Titan Intelligence generates invoice summaries, AI Voice Agents handle inbound calls, Scheduling Pro / Dispatch Pro automates assignment (reported 2× dispatcher capacity). Titan Intelligence prompts are now exposed as custom fields in invoice emails. **May 2026 commercial product update** adds: (a) **AI Financial Reports** — natural-language Q&A over project / business-unit / invoice financial data, returning structured reports without routing through a data analyst. (b) **Bulk Service Agreement renewals** — select a group of expiring agreements, apply a price adjustment across all of them, and move them to renewed in one workflow; HVAC, refrigeration, fire-and-life-safety, and electrical maintenance contracts called out by name as the target use case. (c) **Credit memos** that apply directly to outstanding invoices (eliminates manual reconciliation). Vendor-published Voice Agent case point: a customer booked 500+ jobs / 23% of total monthly bookings via the AI voice agent with no added CSR headcount (Angie Snow, ServiceTitan, AHR 2026); overall 70% booking rate, up to 90% when capacity is available. Bluon for Business + PartsConnect now embedded directly in ServiceTitan (2026-05-22) — see Bluon entry. Implication for skills: bulk-renewal automation is the operational layer adjacent to `sales/maintenance-agreement-writer.md`; the skill remains the cleanest entry point for *drafting* the agreement language, while ServiceTitan handles the *bulk-execution* layer for ST customers. AI Financial Reports is a back-office natural-language query layer that does not displace any current KRASA skill but should be kept in view if a future `admin/finance-question-answerer` skill is considered.
- **ServiceTrade (Stella AI Agents)** — Dominant FSM for commercial fire, life safety, and mechanical contracting. Launched Stella AI Agents on 2026-05-05 (Early Access) — a suite of AI agents built on "Trade Intelligence," a proprietary data layer spanning 14 years of commercial service history and 48 million managed assets. Two agents available at launch: (1) **Stella Quote** — assembles structured deficiency-quote drafts inside the ServiceTrade workflow (asset details, parts, labor, scope language, pricing rules enforced consistently). Cuts turnaround from days/weeks to minutes; ServiceTrade data shows 33% increase in quote approval rates. (2) **Stella Schedule** — optimizes technician scheduling for billable-hours maximization (skill matching, compliance requirements, asset history). Additional agents planned across the service lifecycle including technician preparation. Implication for skills: Stella Quote's pattern (structured deficiency-quote from asset history + pricing rules + scope language) validates the commercial-variants backlog; a future `sales/commercial-deficiency-quote-narrative.md` skill could generate the human-communication narrative ("why this repair matters" framing for building owners) on top of Stella's automated output.
- **BuildOps** — Native Bluon integration (March 2026). Strong commercial and multi-trade focus. On 2026-05-07 launched **Projects**, an AI-native module that extends the platform from service-only into multi-month construction execution (kickoff → closeout) for the same specialty contractors who already run service on BuildOps (~1,500+ at launch). Projects bundles five connected solutions — resource planning, field execution, document control, materials/subcontracts, and project financials — under one embedded AI, **OpsAI**, marketed around surfacing margin slips in the week they happen rather than three months later when a pay app comes back light. Implication for skills: commercial HVAC contractors who run both service and design-build / retrofit work now have one AI-coordinated system of record across the lifecycle; future commercial-variant skill outputs (e.g., `operations/predictive-maintenance-summary.md` commercial variant, a possible `sales/commercial-deficiency-quote-narrative.md`) can reference BuildOps as the single source for both service-call asset history and project-level cost/margin data.
- **Housecall Pro** — AI-generated job summaries, SMS follow-up automation, marketing workflows.
- **Jobber** — AI copilot for quotes, invoices, and customer messaging.
- **FieldEdge** — AI dispatch suggestions, mobile technician AI assist.
- **Workiz** — AI dispatch + route optimization; marketed as cutting scheduling time 40–60%.
- **FieldCamp** — Launched FieldCamp AI Dispatcher in March 2026 for trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical). Skills matching, route optimization, emergency reshuffling. Positioned against the "whiteboard and spreadsheet" legacy of ~30M US field-service workers.
- **Simpro Group (Lightning + Cooper)** — Launched 2026-05-13. AI-native operating platform rolling out across Simpro Group's three FSM products: Simpro (Australia, New Zealand, North America, UK), AroFlo (Australia / New Zealand), and BigChange (UK / Europe). Centered on **Cooper**, an "AI operating layer" pitched as a virtual business partner that surfaces issues early, streamlines internal communication, and learns each contractor's operating model. Ships with four named AI agents at launch — **FieldReady** (technician onboarding compression — vendor-cited reduction from 12–16 weeks to days), **JobReady** (pre-visit preparation aimed at lifting first-time-fix-rate above the 75% industry average toward 90%+), **JobScribe** (field-to-record documentation capture), and **JobBrief** (post-visit customer-summary generation). Public roadmap commits to 20+ specialist agents across the field-service workforce. Implication for skills: Simpro's named-agent surface area overlaps directly with four KRASA skills already in the library — `operations/tech-onboarding-curriculum.md` (FieldReady), the planned dispatcher morning-huddle briefing skill on the backlog (JobReady), `operations/field-report-dictation.md` (JobScribe), and `customer-service/after-hours-call-handler.md` post-call summary patterns (JobBrief). For Simpro / AroFlo / BigChange customers, the AI agents may be the in-FSM execution layer for the same workflows KRASA skills produce as Claude / ChatGPT prompts; for non-Simpro customers, the skills remain the cleanest entry point. Watch item: 12+ month outcome data on FieldReady's 12–16-weeks-to-days claim and JobReady's 75% → 90%+ first-time-fix-rate claim — if those land in published case studies, propagate the benchmarks into `tech-onboarding-curriculum.md` (training-time savings) and the proposed pre-visit-briefing skill (first-time-fix-rate framing).

## Foundation AI Platforms & Cross-Vertical SMB Suites

These are general-purpose AI platforms — not HVAC-vertical — that show up in HVAC contractor stacks as the substrate the more specialized tools sit on top of, or as horizontal SMB skill suites that may overlap with HVAC-vertical skills at the operations / finance / marketing seams.

- **Anthropic Claude for Small Business** — Launched 2026-05-13. Ships with 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows and 15 reusable skills covering finance, operations, sales, marketing, HR, and customer service, plus native connectors to QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack. HVAC shops are named as one of the explicit launch-target verticals (alongside landscapers and brokerages). Workflows include payroll planning against QuickBooks cash and incoming PayPal settlements, month-end close (reconciliation + plain-English P&L + accountant close packet), invoice chasing, campaign planning, and a small-business operations brief. Ships at no additional cost for existing Pro, Max, and Teams subscribers; sensitive actions (send, post, pay) require explicit user approval. **Implication for HVAC vertical skills**: the CFSB skill pack is horizontal (the same payroll / close / invoice-chasing patterns work for any SMB), and KRASA's hvac-ai-skills are vertical (HVAC-specific service-diagnosis, A2L refrigerant explainer, repair-vs-replace, load-calculation, after-hours triage, etc.). The two are complementary, not competing: a contractor running CFSB for back-office can stack a vertical HVAC skill pack on top for trade-specific work. Practical knock-on for skill authoring: where a KRASA skill overlaps a CFSB skill area (e.g., a future `admin/invoice-followup` or `admin/month-end-close` skill), the KRASA version must add measurable HVAC-vertical value (industry pricing rules, manufacturer-tariff context, refrigerant-cost line-item handling, AHRI / Manual J references) or the SMB suite is the better default. Watch item: monitor the CFSB skill list for additions that overlap any KRASA admin/ or `_shared/` skill — if a CFSB skill becomes the strict superset, retire or repoint the KRASA equivalent.
- **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.8 + Dynamic Workflows** — Released 2026-05-28 (41 days after Opus 4.7). Substrate-tier upgrade for any KRASA skill executed against Claude. Three contractor-relevant capabilities: (1) **Opus 4.8** — Anthropic's most capable publicly available model, vendor-cited as ~4× less likely than Opus 4.7 to let coding flaws slip through unflagged; effort controls on claude.ai let users dial how much reasoning Claude puts into a task. (2) **Dynamic Workflows** (research preview) — Claude Code can be asked to *create a workflow* which then orchestrates tens to hundreds of parallel subagents, capped at 1,000 subagents per workflow; framed for codebase-scale migrations from kickoff to merge against an existing test suite. (3) **Cheaper fast mode** — Opus 4.8 fast mode runs at 2.5× the speed of standard mode at $10/M input + $50/M output, roughly 3× cheaper than fast mode on earlier Opus releases. Implication for skills: below skill-rewrite threshold today (KRASA skills are prompt-text + config templates that run on whatever model the contractor uses). Dynamic Workflows is the substrate that could host a contractor's chained KRASA-skill pipeline (e.g., post-visit JobScribe-style report → JobBrief-style customer summary → invoice-followup → review-responder) without the contractor manually wiring each step. Watch item: first published example of a contractor stack chaining 3+ KRASA-style skills inside a single Dynamic Workflow on their own ServiceTitan / BuildOps data, plus any documented per-workflow cost envelopes under the new fast-mode pricing.
- **OpenAI ChatGPT Workspace Agents + ChatGPT for Excel/Sheets** — Workspace Agents announced 2026-04-27, gradually rolling out to ChatGPT Business / Enterprise / Edu workspaces (research preview); free until 2026-05-06, then credit-based. Successor to custom GPTs for org settings: teams build shared agents that handle multi-step workflows under org-defined permissions, run on a schedule or react to Slack messages, with first surfaces in ChatGPT and Slack and more "coming soon" (Salesforce-class integrations). Codex-powered. ChatGPT for Excel and Google Sheets is GA globally for ChatGPT Business as of May 2026 — a spreadsheet-native sidebar for building, cleaning, updating, and explaining workbooks; free preview through 2026-06-02, after which usage follows plan credits. Implication for HVAC vertical skills: same complementarity argument as CFSB — horizontal back-office surface (deal briefs, account research, scheduled summaries piped into Slack) that does not displace KRASA vertical skills; the spreadsheet sidebar is a candidate execution layer for any KRASA skill whose contractor-side artifact lives in Excel (commission rollups, fleet roster maintenance for `maintenance-agreement-writer.md` commercial variants, equipment-inventory audits). Watch item: HVAC-named example or case study in the OpenAI small-business follow-up announcement that's been telegraphed since May 18.
- **Anthropic Claude Managed Agents — Self-Hosted Sandboxes + MCP Tunnels** — Shipped 2026-05-19 at Code with Claude London. Two enterprise-security capabilities that close a long-running watch item ("Claude Managed Agents contractor-vertical availability"). **Self-hosted sandboxes** (public beta): the sandbox in which a Claude agent executes tool calls runs on the customer's own infrastructure (or managed compute providers Cloudflare, Daytona, Modal, Vercel) — orchestration / context management / error recovery stays on Anthropic's side while tool execution moves under the customer's runtime and security controls. **MCP tunnels** (research preview, request access): a lightweight gateway makes a single outbound connection from the customer's private network so Claude agents can reach internal databases, private APIs, knowledge bases, and ticketing systems as MCP tools without exposing them to the public internet (no inbound firewall rules, end-to-end encrypted). **Implication for HVAC vertical skills**: this is what makes Claude Managed Agents practical for HVAC contractors serving security-sensitive commercial verticals — healthcare campuses, data centers, federal / DoD facilities, K-12, regulated manufacturing — where ServiceTitan / FSM data and BMS / BAS APIs are not allowed off-premise. Below skill-rewrite threshold today (infrastructure layer, not prompt-content layer), but unblocks a future tier of KRASA skills that could touch protected building data (occupant PII, energy-use telemetry under SOC 2 / HIPAA / FedRAMP scope) inside a contractor's managed-agent deployment. Watch item: first published HVAC contractor case study running KRASA-style vertical skills through Managed Agents with MCP tunnels back into ServiceTitan / BuildOps / on-prem BMS.
